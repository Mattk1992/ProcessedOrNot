# ProcessedOrNot Scanner - LinkedIn Company Profile

## Company Overview

ProcessedOrNot Scanner is a cutting-edge technology company revolutionizing how consumers understand food processing levels through AI-powered analysis and advanced scanning capabilities. We combine artificial intelligence, computer vision, and comprehensive nutritional databases to provide instant, accurate assessments of food products' processing levels and nutritional value.

## Our Mission

To empower consumers with transparent, science-based insights about their food choices, helping them make informed decisions that support healthier lifestyles. We believe everyone deserves access to clear, actionable information about what they eat.

## What Makes Us Unique

**AI-Powered Analysis**: Our proprietary system uses OpenAI's GPT-4o to analyze ingredient lists and provide processing scores from 0-10, along with detailed explanations of why foods are classified at specific processing levels.

**Multi-Database Integration**: We aggregate data from 14+ authoritative food databases including OpenFoodFacts, USDA FoodData Central, Health Canada, European Food Safety Authority, and regional databases across multiple countries.

**Advanced Scanning Technology**: Our platform supports 6+ barcode formats with real-time camera scanning using ZXing library, plus text-based product search capabilities for comprehensive product discovery.

**Voice-Activated Search**: Industry-leading voice transcription powered by Assembly AI enables hands-free product lookup, making our platform accessible and convenient for all users.

**Global Accessibility**: Built-in internationalization supporting 7 languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Dutch) with dynamic language detection.

**Comprehensive Security**: Enterprise-grade AES-256-CBC encryption for all user data, bcrypt password hashing, and secure session management protecting user privacy.
